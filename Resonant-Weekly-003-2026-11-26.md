## 01 — Community discussions

### Hardware longevity

What if our hardware becomes more useful as it gets older? In both Academy and AI Stack, Manolo explored how advances in models and inference software can increase the capabilities of hardware we already own.

[Academy highlight – 10:05](https://youtu.be/nxTlLemE3oI?t=605) · [AI Stack – 30:45](https://youtu.be/9vFIfs-T2Fk?t=1845) · [Discord discussion](https://discord.com/channels/1423225702135894111/1423225703637323830/1572833201465069580)

### Inklink 2 Mini vs. Qwen 27B

Members shared experiences with inference speed, token efficiency, context windows and instruction following. Early results were promising, although opinions differed regarding coding quality.

[Watch the short edit](https://youtu.be/nxTlLemE3oI) · [Discord discussion](https://discord.com/channels/1423225702135894111/1491424406595964958/1573442427316871169) · [Contrasting experiences](https://discord.com/channels/1423225702135894111/1491424406595964958/1574532721013817355)

### Loop Transformers

Academy explored how repeatedly processing information through the same transformer layers can increase computational depth without proportionally increasing model size.

[Architecture explained – 02:07](https://youtu.be/nxTlLemE3oI?t=127) · [GLM 5.4 and scaling – 03:17](https://youtu.be/nxTlLemE3oI?t=197)

### Strix Halo and inference engines

Members compared Halogen and Crucible, exploring single-session speed, concurrent workloads and context caching. Academy also discussed surprisingly competitive performance on Strix Halo using customized inference engines.

[Academy highlight – 04:12](https://youtu.be/TPHsNxplAHY?t=252) · [Discord benchmarks](https://discord.com/channels/1423225702135894111/1573055465527578696/1573055765206536213) · [AI Stack: Inference optimization – 05:05](https://youtu.be/9vFIfs-T2Fk?t=305)

### Agent harnesses

Can the same model become a better coding agent through changes to its harness? Members explored a Qwen 27B adapter designed specifically for agentic coding workflows.

[Harnesscraft discussion](https://discord.com/channels/1423225702135894111/1546522589852340280/1573915391908913242) · [Community-shared video](https://youtu.be/7asgk8dwCHw)

### Autonomous software factories

A member demonstrated a self-building software factory reporting over 1,700 agent hours of development. The discussion raised fundamental questions about runaway bug creation, human verification, trust and the difference between activity and useful work.

[Software factory highlight](https://youtu.be/TPHsNxplAHY) · [Human verification – 01:38](https://youtu.be/TPHsNxplAHY?t=98) · [Full Academy debate on oversight – 56:30](https://youtu.be/YJ6GE3LsHDU?t=3390)

### Getting started with local AI

New members brought practical questions about affordable GPUs, hardware upgrade cycles, existing equipment and model routing. One recurring recommendation was to start with a local harness before investing heavily in hardware.

[Discord hardware discussion](https://discord.com/channels/1423225702135894111/1423225703637323830/1573863321554391161) · [Choosing GPUs – 35:05](https://youtu.be/9vFIfs-T2Fk?t=2105) · [Older GPUs – 59:12](https://youtu.be/9vFIfs-T2Fk?t=3552) · [Model routing – 1:14:05](https://youtu.be/9vFIfs-T2Fk?t=4445)

## 02 — What members are building

### Prompt sequencing

An ongoing experiment in getting more out of smaller models by breaking complex tasks into sequences of prompts. Open WebUI is the most mature implementation, Pi WebUI is being tested, and a Hermes version awaits testing. An experimental Unreal Engine workflow is also underway.

[Watch the project introduction – 1:26:45](https://youtu.be/9vFIfs-T2Fk?t=5205) · [Follow the experiment on Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1423225702135894111/1491424406595964958/1575242757969027112)

### Physical interfaces and robotics

Members are experimenting with ESP32 microcontrollers, portable voice interfaces, modular sensors and robots connected to their personal AI agents.

[Edge hardware unboxing – 05:30](https://youtu.be/TPHsNxplAHY?t=330) · [Physical interface projects – 1:16:00](https://youtu.be/YJ6GE3LsHDU?t=4560)

### A simulated world for AI perception

A member demonstrated a lightweight simulation environment intended to support robotic perception, spatial learning and the gradual construction of environmental knowledge.

[Watch the demonstration – 1:26:15](https://youtu.be/YJ6GE3LsHDU?t=5175)

### Voice-controlled agents

A member shared progress on integrating speech recognition, language models, skills and MCP into a personal voice-controlled agent.

[Discord project update](https://discord.com/channels/1423225702135894111/1423225703637323830/1575138170939510895)
